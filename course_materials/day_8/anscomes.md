# What and why are Anscombes Quartets.
Anscombe's Quartets were created by the British statistician Francis Anscombe in
1973. The quartets consist of four datasets that have nearly identical simple
descriptive statistics (such as mean, variance, and correlation), but when
graphed, they reveal very different distributions and relationships.

The purpose of Anscombe's Quartets is to demonstrate the importance of visualizing data before analyzing it. Despite having similar statistical properties, the datasets show distinct patterns when plotted, highlighting how relying solely on summary statistics can be misleading. The quartets underscore the value of graphical analysis in understanding the true nature of data.
