## Histograms

### 1. Introduction to Histograms
   - **Definition and Purpose**
     - What is a histogram?
     - Why are histograms useful in data analysis?
   - **Components of a Histogram**
     - X-axis (Number line of the numeric variable)
     - Y-axis (Frequency or Count)
     - Bins (Intervals)
     - Bars (Height representing the number of values within each bin)

### 2. Interpreting Histograms
   - **Understanding Distribution Shapes**
     - Symmetric (Normal distribution)
     - Skewed (Left and Right Skewness)
     - Uniform distribution
     - Bimodal and Multimodal distributions
   - **Identifying Key Features**
     - Center (Mean, Median, Mode)
     - Spread (Range)
     - Outliers
   - **Impact of Bin Width on Interpretation**

### 3. Seaborn
   - **Basic Seaborn Plotting Functions**
     - `sns.histplot()`: Creating histograms

### 4. Creating Histograms with Seaborn
   - **Practical Examples**
     - Plotting a basic histogram
     - Customizing bin width and number of bins
     - Adding labels and titles
   - **Interpreting Seaborn Histograms**
     - Understanding output
     - Analyzing shapes and distributions
   - **Comparing Multiple Data Sets**
     - Overlaying histograms

### 5. Hands-On Practice
   - Students create their own histograms using sample data sets.
   - Explore different bin sizes and their impact.
   - Identify the shape, center, and spread of their data sets.
